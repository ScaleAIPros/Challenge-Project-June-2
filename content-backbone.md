## 7 Key Points

### 1. What HeyGen Actually Is (And Why It Matters for Your Business)
HeyGen lets you type a video idea and get back a finished video with a
lifelike AI presenter — called an avatar — reading your script, on camera,
with music and captions. No filming. No editing. No studio.

**Why it matters:** You can create marketing videos, explainers, and social
content in minutes instead of days — for a fraction of the cost.

---

### 2. AI Agent: Describe It, Get a Video
From HeyGen's home screen, you type what you want your video to be about.
The AI builds the full video for you — script, visuals, voice, and timing —
all from one description.

**The secret:** Be specific. The more detail you give, the better the result.
Include the tone ("confident and motivating"), the audience ("beginners"),
and the goal ("teach people how to grow on YouTube").

**Practical example:** A business owner could type: *"Create a 60-second
explainer video for my bookkeeping service. Show small business owners
feeling relieved, not stressed, about their finances. Keep the tone warm
and professional."* → Done.

---

### 3. The Editor: You Stay in Control
After the AI builds your video, you can refine every detail inside the
AI Studio editor:
- **Script:** Click any scene and retype. Make it tighter, more direct, more you.
- **Captions:** Change the font, size, color, and style to match your brand.
- **Background:** Upload your own image OR use the built-in AI image generator
  to describe and create a custom background.
- **Motion:** Set how energetic your avatar looks — calm, welcoming, excited.
  (Tip: avoid "expressive motion" unless you want some... creative gestures.)

---

### 4. Clone Yourself — Your AI Twin Does the Work
HeyGen can create a digital twin of you from a 15-second webcam or phone recording.
Once created, your AI clone can:
- Read any script you write in your voice and likeness
- Appear in any video you generate going forward
- Change outfits and environments without re-recording

**The business value:** Record yourself once. Use it forever. Your clone can
produce daily content while you run your business.

---

### 5. One Video, Infinite Looks
After creating a video with your clone, you can change the visual style
completely without re-recording:
- Swap to a different background (cinematic, office, outdoor)
- Change your outfit with an AI prompt ("dark fitted jacket, spotlight")
- Adjust the entire mood and aesthetic — same script, totally new feel

Great for repurposing content across different platforms and audiences.

---

### 6. Speak Every Language — Automatically
HeyGen translates your videos into 175+ languages with a few clicks.
Upload your English video, choose a target language (French, Spanish,
Japanese, etc.), and HeyGen re-dubs it with your avatar speaking the new
language naturally.

**Business impact:** One video, global reach. No translator. No re-recording.

---

### 7. Go Faceless — Animated Characters for Any Channel
Don't want to be on camera at all? Create a fully animated AI character
with a custom personality, voice, and look. You can build:
- An educational mascot for your brand
- A faceless content channel
- A story-driven series

The character can take on unlimited looks and styles, making it easy to
scale content across multiple audiences and niches.

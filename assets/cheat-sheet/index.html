HeyGen Quick-Start
Cheat Sheet

Everything you need to make your first AI video — one page, plain English, no tech skills required.

01
🎬 What HeyGen Does
Type a video idea → get back a finished video with an AI presenter, script, captions, music, and timing. No camera. No editing. No studio.
02
🤖 AI Agent — Your Shortcut
From the home screen, describe your video. The AI builds everything from one prompt. Be specific: mention tone, audience, and goal for better results.
Example: "60-second explainer for my bookkeeping service, warm and professional tone, targeting small business owners"
03
✏️ The Editor — Stay in Control
Click any scene to rewrite it. Change captions style, generate AI backgrounds by description, set avatar energy level (calm / welcoming / excited).
⚠️ Avoid "expressive motion" — it makes avatars move erratically.
04
🧬 Clone Yourself (15 sec)
Avatar tab → New Avatar → Clone a real person. Record 15 sec via webcam or phone. Turn on: 4K, noise removal. Your AI twin is then ready for unlimited videos.
05
🎨 Change Looks Instantly
Edit in AI Studio → Avatar → New Look → Design with AI. Describe outfit + environment. Applies to every scene. Same script, brand new aesthetic.
06
🌍 175+ Languages, Zero Re-recording
Home → Translate → Browse local files → Choose language → Translate. Your avatar speaks the new language naturally. No translator needed.
07
✨ Go Faceless — Animated Characters
Avatar tab → New Avatar → Create virtual character → Design with AI. Name it, set style (Pixar / realistic), describe its world. Add a voice from the library. Use it to build a faceless channel, educational mascot, or story-driven series — no camera ever needed.

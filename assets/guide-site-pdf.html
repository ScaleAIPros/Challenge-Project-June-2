The 7 Things You Need to Know

Every key point explained in plain English

01 🎬
What HeyGen Actually Is
Turn a text prompt into a finished video — no camera needed
The Tool

HeyGen lets you type your video idea and get back a polished, professional video with a lifelike AI presenter reading your script. No filming. No editing. No studio. Just describe what you want, and HeyGen builds the whole thing: script, visuals, voice, captions, and timing.

💡 Business owners are using HeyGen to create marketing explainers, onboarding videos, and social content in minutes — not days.
02 🤖
AI Agent: Describe It, Get a Video
One prompt → full video, automatically
The Shortcut

From HeyGen's home screen, describe what you want. The AI Agent builds your complete video from a single description — choosing the structure, writing the script, picking visuals, and syncing the voice. The secret is being specific: mention the tone, audience, and goal in your prompt.

💡 Try: "Create a 60-second explainer for my bookkeeping service. Show small business owners feeling relieved about their finances. Tone: warm and professional."
03 ✏️
The Editor: You Stay in Control
AI builds the draft — you make it perfect
The Control Room

After the AI builds your video, jump into AI Studio to refine every detail. Click any scene to rewrite the script. Change caption style to match your brand. Generate a custom background by describing it in plain English. Set the avatar's energy level — calm, welcoming, or excited.

💡 Avoid the 'expressive motion' toggle unless you want your avatar to start doing... interpretive dance.
04 🧬
Clone Yourself — Once and Done
15 seconds of recording → your AI twin that works forever
The Clone

HeyGen can create a digital twin of you from a short webcam or phone recording. Once created, your AI clone reads any script in your voice and likeness, appears in any video you generate, and can change outfits and environments without you ever recording again.

💡 Record once. Let your clone produce content while you run your business. That's the real leverage.
05 🎨
One Video, Infinite Looks
Repurpose content across platforms without re-recording
The Multiplier

After creating a video with your clone, change the entire visual style with an AI prompt — different background, different outfit, different vibe — without touching the script or re-recording. Same content, totally new feel. Perfect for repurposing one video across LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube.

💡 Prompt example: "Dark cinematic stage, soft spotlight, subtle rim lighting, dark fitted jacket." Instant makeover.
06 🌍
Speak Every Language — Automatically
175+ languages, no re-recording, no translator
The Translator

Upload your English video, choose a target language (French, Spanish, Japanese — 175+ options), and HeyGen re-dubs it with your avatar speaking naturally in that language. One video, global reach. No translator needed. No re-recording.

💡 You might not speak French. But your avatar can. That's the kind of scale that used to cost tens of thousands of dollars.
07 ✨
Go Faceless — Animated Characters
Build a content channel without ever being on camera
The Faceless Path

Don't want to be on camera? Create a fully animated AI character with a custom personality, voice, and look. Give it a name, choose its style (Pixar, realistic, cartoon), describe its world, and start generating content. You can build an educational mascot, a faceless content channel, or a story-driven series.

💡 One animated character with unlimited looks = one channel that can scale to multiple audiences and niches without you ever pressing record.

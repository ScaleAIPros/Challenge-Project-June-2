The 7 Things You Need to Know
01 HeyGen creates videos without filmingTurn a text prompt into a polished video with an AI presenter, visuals, voice, captions, and timing.
02 One prompt can build an entire videoBe specific about audience, goal, tone, and length. The AI Agent builds the first draft.
03 You stay in controlUse AI Studio to edit scenes, rewrite scripts, adjust captions, change backgrounds, and match your brand.
04 Clone yourself onceRecord a short sample, then let your digital twin read future scripts in your likeness and voice.

Simple Step-by-Step Guide: Create Your First HeyGen Video
1 Choose one clear goalPick one outcome: explain a service, answer a common question, introduce your business, or follow up with leads.
2 Write your promptUse this formula: Audience + problem + solution + tone + length. Example: "Create a 60-second explainer for small business owners about reducing missed calls. Tone: warm and professional."
3 Generate the first draftOpen HeyGen, start with the AI Agent, paste your prompt, and let it create the script, scenes, voice, captions, and visuals.
4 Review the script firstMake sure the message sounds like your business. Fix unclear lines, remove fluff, and add your call to action.
5 Pick the presenter styleUse your own avatar, a stock avatar, or a faceless animated character. Choose the option that fits your comfort level and brand.
6 Customize the lookAdd your logo, brand colors, captions, background, and preferred visual style. Keep it clean and easy to read.
7 Preview and polishWatch the full video. Check pronunciation, pacing, captions, transitions, and CTA. Make small edits before exporting.
8 Publish and repurposeExport the video. Post it on one platform first, then create alternate versions for email, website, LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube.
